# SLA by Severity

document_id: KB-002
topic: response and resolution targets by severity

For P1, the initial response is due within 15 minutes and the target resolution time is within 4 hours.
For P2, the initial response is due within 1 hour and the target resolution time is within 12 hours.
For P3, the initial response is due within 4 hours and the target resolution time is within 48 hours.
For P4, the initial response is due within 12 hours and the target resolution time is within 72 hours.
The initial response SLA measures when support first replies, while the resolution target measures the intended time to resolve the issue.
