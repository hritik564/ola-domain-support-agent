# VIP Customer Handling

document_id: KB-010
topic: VIP support prioritisation and safeguards

VIP status changes response prioritisation but does not override safety, refund, privacy, or fraud controls.
Qualifying P2 or P3 VIP tickets may be routed to a specialist queue.
Do not give VIP customers unsupported compensation or confidential internal information.
VIP priority treatment must remain auditable.
