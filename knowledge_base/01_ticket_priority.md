# Ticket Priority Classification

document_id: KB-001
topic: ticket-priority classification rules

P1 Critical applies to a service-wide outage, an account-security incident, or a payment charge when the service is completely unavailable.
P2 High applies when a customer cannot access an account, payment repeatedly fails, or a serious malfunction affects one customer.
P3 Medium applies to a delayed refund, a billing dispute, or a repeated complaint without active service loss.
P4 Low applies to general information, feedback, and non-urgent clarification requests.
Assign severity based on operational impact, urgency, and the number of affected users.
