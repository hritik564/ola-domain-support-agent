# Escalation Matrix

document_id: KB-003
topic: severity-based escalation paths

Escalate P1 tickets immediately to the Incident Manager.
Escalate a P2 ticket to the Support Lead if it remains unresolved after 6 hours.
Escalate a P3 ticket if it remains unresolved after 36 hours or the customer contacts support 3 times about the same unresolved issue.
P4 tickets normally remain with first-line support unless policy or legal review is required.
Automated escalation recommendations do not replace human ownership of a ticket.
