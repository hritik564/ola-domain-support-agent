# Business Hours and Holiday Support

document_id: KB-006
topic: support desk hours and holiday coverage

The standard support desk operates from 08:00 to 22:00 IST every day.
P1 critical incidents are monitored 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
P2 through P4 requests submitted outside standard support hours remain queued according to severity.
Published holidays do not suspend P1 incident handling.
