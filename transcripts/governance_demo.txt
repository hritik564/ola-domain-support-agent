Batch D governance demo
Risk classification: MEDIUM
Rationale:
- Customer-support decisions affect user experience, and ticket escalation recommendations may affect operational handling.
- The system cannot issue refunds or payments, modify accounts, or close tickets automatically; write access is intentionally absent.
- A human/operator can review outcomes. Groundedness checks, PII masking, sanitized API errors, and audit logs reduce the remaining risk.

Agent tool-access matrix:
- Retrieval Agent: rag_lookup
- Lookup Agent: ticket_status_lookup
- Response Composer: no tools
- Policy Compliance Reviewer: no tools
- Final Editor: no tools

Prohibited capabilities:
- ticket mutation
- refund or compensation issuance
- account changes
- external communication
- billing or payment actions

Least-autonomy check: passed.
Reviewer/editor operational tools: none.
